## Where the definitions differ

- **Passes.** StatsBomb logs every pass, set pieces included, as a `Pass` event. Wyscout records set pieces (throw-ins, goal kicks, corners, free kicks) as `Free Kick` events. SPADL maps both onto the same pass types. socceraction also drops StatsBomb passes with outcome Injury Clearance or Unknown.
- **Shots.** Wyscout splits direct free-kick shots and penalties into `Free Kick` subtypes; StatsBomb keeps them as `Shot` events with a type.
- **Goals.** Both providers credit own goals separately (StatsBomb as paired `Own Goal For` and `Own Goal Against` events, Wyscout as tag 102). Goals agree with the score in both providers (checks `spadl_goals_equal_score` and `wy_goals_equal_score`).
- **Fouls.** Wyscout's `Foul` covers more than fouls committed in play: hand fouls, protests, simulation, time-wasting and late cards are all `Foul` subtypes.
- **Tackles.** StatsBomb records a tackle as a `Duel` of type Tackle (attempted tackles, won or lost). Wyscout has no tackle type; the closest raw equivalent is the ground defending duel, which also covers duels without a tackle. socceraction derives SPADL tackles from each provider differently.
